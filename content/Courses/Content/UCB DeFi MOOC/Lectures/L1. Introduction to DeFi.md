## Introduction

- Finance 
	- is the process that involves the creation, management, and investment of money and financial assets.
	- Financial assets/financial instruments:
		- a non-physical asset whose value is derived from a contractual claim
		- Bank deposits, stocks, bonds, loans, derivatives
	- Financial services include banking, lending/borrowing, securities, insurance, trusts, funds
	- Financial markets: marketplace for trading financial assets
- Brief History of Web3
