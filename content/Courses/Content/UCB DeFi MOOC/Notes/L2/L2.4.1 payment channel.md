- **Hashed TimeLock Contract** (**HTLC**) Logic: Two ways to close channel:
	- Tx with Alice sig: can close channel after 30 days
	- Tx with Alice sig & Bob sig: close channel right away
- Example: Alice go to Bob's coffee shop everyday to grab a cup of coffee.
	- Instead of paying to Bob everyday, Alice creates a payment channel to Bob with value of $100 (e.g., UTXO or DAPP).
		- The creation costs gas fee, i.e. on-chain.
	- One day Alice wants to pay Bob $5, then she can creates a transaction
		`Tx: distribute funds: Alice: 95; Bob: 5; sig_Alice`
		- Off-chain message
	- Bob can sign Tx and close channel, but he can also wait (up to 30 days).
	- Finally they would like to close the channel with Alice sig & Bob sig.
		- The closure also costs gas fee.
	- Note: Participants only touch chain when a channel is created or closed.
- **Payment Networks**: Network of payment channels
	- If Alice wants to pay Bob, she will automatically find the lowest cost path.
	- In reality, it will try multiple paths at the same time to ensure final success.
- Payment channels are necessary to enable state-wide adoption.
