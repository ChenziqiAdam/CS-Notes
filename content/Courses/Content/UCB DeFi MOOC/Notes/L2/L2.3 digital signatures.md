- A signature scheme is a triple of algorithms:
	- **Gen**(): outputs a key pair (pk, sk)
	- **Sign**(sk, msg) outputs sig. $\sigma$
	- **Verify**(pk, msg, $\sigma$) outputs 'accept' or 'reject'
- Familiy of Signature Schemes
	1. RSA signatures (not used in blockchains): long sigs and public keys (≥256 bytes), fast to verify
	2. Discrete-log signatures: Schnorr and ECDSA
		- short sigs (48 or 64 bytes) and public keys (32 bytes)
		- Bitcoin, Ethereum
	3. BLS signatures: 48 bytes, aggregatable, easy threshold
		- Ethereum 2.0, Chia, Dfinity
	4. Post-quantum signatures: No worry for quantum computers
