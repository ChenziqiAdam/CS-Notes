- Also called "Hierarchical Aggregation"
- For each aggregate function, need a global/local decomposition:
	- $\text{sum}(S) = \Sigma \Sigma (s)$
	- $\text{count} = \Sigma \text{count}(s)$
	- $\text{avg}(S) = (\Sigma \Sigma (s)) / \Sigma \text{count}(s)$
