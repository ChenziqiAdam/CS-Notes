- Q. 2PL cannot avoid cascading aborts, which is a scenario where the abort of one transaction requires the abort of another transaction.
	- Example: **rollback** of T1 requires **rollback** of T2.
		![[Pasted image 20250801101529.png]]
- Strict 2PL
	- Same as 2PL, except *all* locks released together when transaction completes
	- (i.e.) either
		- Transaction has committed (all writes durable), OR
		- Transaction has aborted (all writes have been undone)
	![[Pasted image 20250801101648.png|400]]
