- ***Dictionary Comprehension***: similar to *list comprehension*
	- Example: gryffindors with *list comprehension*
		```Python
		students = ["Hermione", "Harry", "Ron"]
		
		gryffindors = [{"name": student, "house": "Gryffindor"} for student in students]
		
		print(greffindors)
```
	- Example: gryffindors with *dictionary comprehension*
		```Python
		students = ["Hermione", "Harry", "Ron"]
		
		gryffindors = {student: "Gryffindor" for student in students} # dictionary comprehension
		
		print(greffindors)
```
