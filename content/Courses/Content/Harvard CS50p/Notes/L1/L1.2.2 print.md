- **==Learn to Read the Official Documentations==**
	**Example**
	- `print(*objects, sep=' ', end='\n', file=sys.stdout, flush=False)`
		- `*` : functions can take in infinite parameters.
		- if you want the print function do not print a new line, just change `end='\n'` to `end=""`
		- `objects`: ***position parameters***
		- `sep`, `end`: ***named parameters***
		- **Conner Case:**
			what if programmers want to print quotes?
				- Method1: use single quotes on the outside, i.e. `print('hello, "friend"')`
				- Method2: use ***escape character***(转义字符): `\`, i.e. `print("hello, \"friend\"")`
