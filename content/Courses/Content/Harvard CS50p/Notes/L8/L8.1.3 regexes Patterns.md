- `\d`: <u>decimal digit</u>
- `\D`: <u>not a decimal digit</u>
- `\s`: <u>whitespace character</u>
- `\S`: <u>not a whitespace character</u>
- `\w`: <u>word character ... as well as numbers and the underscore</u>
- `\W`: <u>not a word character</u>
