- Special symbols for *regexes*
	- `.`: <u>any character except a newline</u>
	- `*`: <u>0 or more repetitions</u>
	- `+`: <u>1 or more repetitions</u>
	- `?`: <u>0 or 1 repetition</u>
	- `{m}`: <u>m repetitions</u>
	- `{m, n}`: <u>m-n repetitions</u>
	- `^`: <u>matches the start of the string</u>
	- `$`: <u>matches the end of the string or just before the newline at the end of the string</u>
