- Number Representation Revisited
	- Given 32 bits, what can we represent so far?
		- Signed and unsigned integers
		- 4 characters (ASCII)
		- Instructions & addresses
	- Q. How to encode the following?
		- Real numbers (e.g. 3.14159)
		- Very large numbers (e.g. 6.02x1023)
		- Very small numbers (e.g. 6.626x10-34)
		- Special numbers (e.g. infinity)
	- Q. Why can't we represent fractions?
		- The lowest power of 2 is 2<sup>0</sup> = 1, so the smallest difference between any two numbers is 1.
		- For decimal (base 10), decimal "point" signifies boundary between integer and fraction parts.
