- Double Precision: 
	- Total bits: Next multiple of word size (64 bits)
		- Sign bit: 1 bit (Bit 63)
		- Exponent: 11 bits (Bits 62-52)
		- Significand (Fraction): 52 bits (Bits 51-0)
	- C variable declared as double
	- Exponent bias is 2<sup>10</sup>-1 = 1023
	- Primary advantage is **greater precision** due to larger Significand (v.s. Single Precision)
