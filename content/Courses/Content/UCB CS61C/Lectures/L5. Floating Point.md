- Summary
	- Floating point approximates real numbers:
		- Largest magnitude: 2<sup>128</sup> - 2<sup>104</sup> (Exp = OxFE)
		- Smallest magnitude: 2<sup>-149</sup> (denorm)
		- Also has encodings for 0, Infinity, NaN
	- Floating point has some limitations:
		- Overflow, underflow, rounding
		- Gaps and distribution of values
		- Associativity
