## Reward Model: Better Evaluation for Alignment


- Traditional Feedback Loop: Provide no internal insights
	![[Pasted image 20260301114203.png]]
	- $\pi$: Policy
	- Agent: LM
	- $a$: Completion
- Reward Model Training
	- Idea:
		- Take in pairwise preferences, the prompt plus one selected completion and one rejected
		- Pass in at the same time and return two output (batch mode)
		![[Pasted image 20260301114501.png]]
	- Loss Function
	$$
	L_{\text{PM}} = \log(1 + e^{r_{\text{rejected}} - r_{\text{chosen}}})
	$$
		- Separate the distance between the two completions and create a margin to learn
- Q. How to evaluate reward models?
	- How do reward models / preference models improve final LLM capabilities?
	- How do reward models encode safety / other specific features?
	- How do scaling laws improve specific properties of reward models?
	- ...
- RM is important for DPO models.
	$$
		r(x, y) = \beta \log \frac{\pi(y|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y|x)} + \beta \log Z(x).
		$$
	- Given two completions to a prompt, we compare the rewards $r(x, y_1)$ and $r(x, y_2)$ as follows, where the score is computed via the log ratios of $\pi$:
	$$
	\log \frac{\pi(y_1|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_1|x)} > \log \frac{\pi(y_2|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_2|x)}.
	$$
	- As the math tells, we need the reference model for building the reward model. 
	- The reference model is a frozen based model, serving as an anchor during alignment training.
		- When optimizing a model with RL or preference learning, the model can drift too far (mode collapse, verbosity hacks, reward hacking).

## DPO or PPO, Online or Offline

- Online data is freshly generated from the policy and/or recently labelled by a reward model / judge.
	- PPO does both with generation + reward model scoring
	- Other methods use different ways for doing this: collect new preference data, re-label existing data, LLM-as-a-judge, reward model ranking
- In short, online is better and the coming training trend.
	- Iterative data collection
	- Short timelines for each iteration
	- Some sort of "distribution shift" per method
	- Hypothesis: Rejection sampling, DPO, then PPO
