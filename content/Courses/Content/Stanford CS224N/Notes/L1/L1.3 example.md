- Objective Function
	- Maximize $J'(\theta) = \prod_{t=1}^{T} \prod_{-m \le j \le m, j \ne 0} p(w_{t+j} | w_t; \theta)$
	- Or minimize avg. neg log likelihood $J(\theta) = -\frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \sum_{-m \le j \le m, j \ne 0} \log p(w_{t+j} | w_t)$
		*(Annotations: "negate to minimize; log is monotone" pointing to the negative sign. "$T$" is the "Text length". "$m$" is the "window size".)*
	- Where $p(o|c) = \frac{\exp(u_o^T v_c)}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)}$
		*(Annotations: "$o$" is the "word idx of output word". "$c$" is the "word idx of center word". The overall fraction is explained as: "Each word type (vocab entry) has two representations: center word and context word".)*
- We now take derivatives to work at minimum.
	$\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log \frac{\exp(u_o^T v_c)}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)}$
	$= \frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log \exp(u_o^T v_c) - \frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log \sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)$
	1. $\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log \exp(u_o^T v_c) = \frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} u_o^T v_c = u_o$
		*(Annotations: "Vectors! Not high school single variable calculus". Also, "You can do things one variable at a time, and this may be helpful when things get gnarly. $\frac{\partial}{\partial v_j(i)} u_o^T v_j = \frac{\partial}{\partial v_j(i)} \sum_k (u_{o,k} v_{j,k}) = u_{o,i}$ Each term is zero except when i=j".)*
	2. $\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log \sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)$
		- Let $f(g(v_c)) = \frac{\partial L}{\partial v_c}$ *(Annotation: Important to change index)*
			$\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log(\sum_{x=1}^V \exp(u_x^T v_c))$ *(Annotation: Use chain rule)*
			$= \frac{1}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)} \cdot (\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \sum_{x=1}^V \exp(u_x^T v_c))$ *(Annotation: Move deriv inside sum)*
			$= \frac{1}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)} \cdot (\sum_{x=1}^V \frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \exp(u_x^T v_c))$ *(Annotation: Chain rule)*
			$= \frac{1}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)} \sum_{x=1}^V \exp(u_x^T v_c) u_x$
			$\frac{\partial}{\partial v_c} \log p(o|c) = u_o - \frac{1}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)} \cdot (\sum_{x=1}^V \exp(u_x^T v_c) u_x)$
			$= u_o - \sum_{x=1}^V \frac{\exp(u_x^T v_c)}{\sum_{w=1}^V \exp(u_w^T v_c)} u_x$ *(Annotation: Distribute term across sum)*
			$= u_o - \sum_{x=1}^V p(x|c) u_x$ *(Annotation: This is an expectation: average value of random vectors weighted by their probability)*
			$= \text{observed} - \text{expected}$
- This is just the derivatives for the center vector parameters. We need derivatives for output vector parameters. (They're similar).
- Then we have derivatives wrt. all parameters and can minimize.
