| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |

| vector | a number with direction (e.g. wind speed with direction) but can also have many other numbers | 1                                                                                     | Lower(`y`)     |
| matrix | a 2-dimensional array of numbers                                                              | 2                                                                                     | Upper (`Q`)    |
