- CNN: [Convolutional Neural Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convolutional_neural_network) (CNN or ConvNet).
	- CNN's are known for their capabilities to find patterns in visual data.
	- The CNN model we use is known as TinyVGG from the [CNN Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/cnn-explainer/) website.
		- It follows the typical structure of a convolutional neural network:
			`Input layer -> [Convolutional layer -> activation layer -> pooling layer] -> Output layer`
			Where the contents of `[Convolutional layer -> activation layer -> pooling layer]` can be upscaled and repeated multiple times, depending on requirements.
