### 0. Motivation for This Series
- All CS Courses kind of mislead you about what programming entails.
	- Fill in the function
	- Or implement the class
	- Or implement the algorithm
- **The greatest limitation we face in building systems is being able to understand what we're building!**
	- Always working at a “small scale” introduces habits that will cause you great pain later.
	- We should learn how to deal with the "large scale".
	- And our most important goal is to keep our software simple.
