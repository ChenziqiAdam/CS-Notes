- Basic idea: Just like LSD, but sort from leftmost digit towards the right.
	- Avoid cases that keys are too long.

- Runtime of MSD
	- Best case: Θ(N + R) (only check the top digit)
	- Worst case: Θ(WN + WR) (degenerate to LSD sort)
