- ***TCP***/***IP***: a pair of protocols(协议)
	get data package from A to B
	- ****IP***: internet protocol
		address: every device on the internet own a unique IP: `#.#.#.#` (IPv4: IP version 4)
		- the core part is the source address and the destination address
		- govern how computers address each other on the internet
	- ***TCP***: transmission control protocol
		memo(备忘录), sequence number, port number
		- Two most common port numbers:
			- 80 `HTTP`
			- 443 `HTTPS`
		- govern how computers keep track of sequences of packets form A to B
	e.g. `1.2.3.4:80`
