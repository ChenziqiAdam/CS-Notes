- <u>Hyperlinks</u>
	- **HTML: hypertext markup language**
	- Example:
		```HTML
		 <!DOCTYPE html>
				
		<html lang="en">
			<head>
				<title>link</title>
			</head>
			<body>
				Visit <a href="https://www.harvard.edu/">Harvard</a>
		    </body>
		 </html>
	```
		- `<a>`: <u>anchor tag</u>
		- `Harvard`: the literal content
			- **Phishing**: the literal content does not match the underneath URL
